# Phase 4: Risk Management Framework

Risk management protects Henry's capital so he can stay in business through all market regimes.

---

## 1. Portfolio Governance Rules

1. **Maximum Drawdown Cutoff**: If total portfolio drawdown reaches **10%** from peak equity, trading size is cut by 50% across all setups.
2. **Daily Loss Limit**: If daily losses reach **3%** of total capital, trading is halted for the remainder of the session (no revenge trading).
3. **Max Concurrent Exposure**: Maximum total open risk across all simultaneous positions capped at **4%** of equity.

---

## 2. Correlation & Exposure Guards

- **Single Asset Concentration**: Since this framework focuses on Bitcoin, risk is concentrated in BTC spot and derivatives. Never double-dip risk by taking overlapping long setups in correlated altcoins simultaneously.
- **Liquidation Buffer**: Perpetual futures entries must maintain a liquidation price at least **30%** away from current price, ensuring market spikes cannot trigger exchange liquidation before structural invalidation is reached.

---

## 3. Psychological & Circuit Breaker Discipline

- **Three Consecutive Losses**: Triggers a mandatory 24-hour cool-off period to review camera feeds and check for regime shifts (e.g. transitioning from +Gamma rotation to -Gamma trend expansion).
- **Execution Log**: Every trade must be logged with the 6-camera alignment state at the exact moment of entry.
