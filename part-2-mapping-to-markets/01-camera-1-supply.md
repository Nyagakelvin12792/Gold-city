# Camera 1 — Supply

## 1. Gold City Narrative

Every morning, Henry looks at Camera 1 to answer a simple question: how much gold is actually moving toward the exchange floor today?

Out in the hills surrounding Gold City, **Alice** operates the gold mines. Her miners dig up fresh gold daily and transport it down the highway into the city. If Alice increases production or decides to dump accumulated inventory onto the market to cover her operational costs, supply on the exchange floors increases.

Deeper inside the city, **Jonas** sits in his ancient stone vault. Jonas holds a massive portion of the city's historical gold wealth. For months or years, Jonas's vault doors remain tightly sealed. But whenever Jonas unseals a vault door and loads gold wagons toward the exchange, Henry takes immediate notice.

---

## 2. Theory

Market prices reflect the balance between supply and demand. Supply comes from two primary sources:
1. **New Issuance**: Ongoing production from network producers (miners).
2. **Dormant Supply Activation**: Long-held assets moving out of cold storage back into active circulation.

When total active liquid supply increases rapidly without an equivalent surge in demand, prices experience downward pressure or ceiling friction. Conversely, when supply is locked away into long-term storage or illiquid entities, available market float shrinks, making price more sensitive to demand inflows.

---

## 3. Real Market Translation

Camera 1 translates into the following trackable Bitcoin metrics:

- **Alice (Miner Issuance & Flow)**:
  - Daily BTC Mining Issuance (~450 BTC/day post-2024 halving)
  - Miner Exchange Inflow Volume
  - Miner Reserve Balances
- **Jonas (Dormant & Long-Term Holder Supply)**:
  - Long-Term Holder (LTH) vs. Short-Term Holder (STH) Supply Ratio
  - Coin Days Destroyed (CDD) and Adjusted CDD
  - HODL Waves (Supply Last Active 1Y+, 3Y+, 5Y+)
- **Exchange Liquid Float**:
  - Total BTC Balance on Spot Exchanges

---

## 4. Litmus Test

"Is supply being actively unlocked and pushed onto exchange floors, or is circulating float continuing to shrink into long-term vaults?"

---

## 5. Update Frequency & Data Source

- **Update Frequency**: Daily to Weekly (supply metrics shift over days and weeks, not seconds).
- **Primary Data Sources**:
  - Glassnode (`Supply in Smart Contracts / Exchanges`, `LTH Supply`, `Adjusted CDD`)
  - CryptoQuant (`Exchange Reserve BTC`, `Miner Outflow`)
  - Checkonchain (`HODL Waves`, `Realized Cap HODL Waves`)
