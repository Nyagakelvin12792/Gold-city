# Phase 2: Recurring Conditions & Setup Definitions

In SVAF, we do not trade patterns or shapes. We trade **recurring auction conditions** where participant incentives align to create predictable structural behavior.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Validation Status-Tag System**
> Every setup defined in this framework carries a strict status tag:
> - `[HYPOTHESIS — NOT YET BACKTESTED]`: Default tag for all setups until empirical backtesting is completed.
> - `[VALIDATED — BACKTESTED]`: Applied only when verified against empirical statistics from the SVAF Python backtesting engine.
> Never claim a win rate or statistical edge without a `[VALIDATED]` tag backing it.

## Setup 1: The Failed Migration Snapback
**Status**: `[HYPOTHESIS — NOT YET BACKTESTED]`

### Narrative Condition
Price attempts to migrate out of an established District (HVN) into an empty Highway (LVN). However, the initiative buyers (David) run out of energy against Frank's massive resting sell wall. No new business takes place at the higher floor, and price snaps violently back into the old district.

### Camera Criteria
1. **Camera 3 (Profile)**: Price extends beyond VAH into LVN space.
2. **Camera 4 (DOM)**: Dense limit sell orders absorb aggressive buyers at the extension high.
3. **Camera 2 (Aggression)**: High futures CVD with flat or falling spot CVD, followed by CVD turning sharply negative.
4. **Camera 5 (Gamma)**: High +Gamma open interest resting back inside the Value Area.

### Actionable Setup
- **Bias**: Short / Mean-reversion back to VPOC.
- **Target**: Session VPOC or Value Area Low (VAL).

## Setup 2: Initiative Highway Breakout
**Status**: `[HYPOTHESIS — NOT YET BACKTESTED]`

### Narrative Condition
Strong spot buyers (Ben & Clara) enter the exchange building and aggressively buy up all of Frank's inventory on the current floor. Finding no resistance, they drive price through an empty Highway (LVN) toward a higher district. Frank is forced to pull his ask quotes higher.

### Camera Criteria
1. **Camera 6 (Weather)**: Macro liquidity (M2) expanding, DXY weakening.
2. **Camera 1 (Supply)**: Spot exchange reserves declining; no incoming miner/Jonas supply.
3. **Camera 3 (Profile)**: Price consolidates at the boundary of VAH, building volume acceptance.
4. **Camera 5 (Gamma)**: Price crosses above the Zero-Gamma Flip level into -Gamma territory.
5. **Camera 2 (Aggression)**: Spot CVD leads futures CVD higher with expanding Open Interest.

### Actionable Setup
- **Bias**: Long / Trend continuation across the LVN.
- **Target**: Next higher High Volume Node (HVN) district.

## Setup 3: Responsive Value Area Rotation
**Status**: `[HYPOTHESIS — NOT YET BACKTESTED]`

### Narrative Condition
The market is in a balanced, quiet state. Price rotates back and forth between the northern border (VAH) and southern border (VAL) of the district. When price reaches VAL, responsive buyers step in to move price back toward the center; when price reaches VAH, responsive sellers step in.

### Camera Criteria
1. **Camera 6 (Weather)**: Neutral macro environment; no scheduled rate announcements or fiscal drains.
2. **Camera 3 (Profile)**: Symmetrical, well-developed bell-curve Volume Profile.
3. **Camera 5 (Gamma)**: Deep +Gamma regime pinning price between strike bounds.
4. **Camera 2 (Aggression)**: Low CVD volume; delta turns positive at VAL and negative at VAH.

### Actionable Setup
- **Bias**: Mean-reversion (Long at VAL, Short at VAH).
- **Target**: Value Area VPOC.
