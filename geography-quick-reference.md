# Atlas — Geography Quick Reference

| Gold City term | Real market equivalent |
|---|---|
| City | The whole BTC market |
| District (downtown / midtown / uptown) | Established HVN zone, higher-timeframe |
| Highway | LVN between districts, fast-crossing empty space |
| Building | A real exchange venue (Binance, Coinbase, Kraken, OKX), each with its own book |
| Floor | A single price level inside a building's order book |
| Frank (per floor) | The resting liquidity / market maker at that specific price level |

**Timeframe is not a location.** It's how zoomed-in the observation is:

- **Weekly** = the whole city from above
- **Daily** = a single district
- **4H** = inside one building
