# Phase 1: Reading All Six Cameras Together — The Alignment Matrix & Decision Funnel

Henry does not make trading decisions based on a single camera. A single camera gives a picture; six cameras together reveal the story.

To process all six camera feeds without getting overwhelmed, Henry uses a **Decision Funnel**.

---

## 2. The Alignment Matrix

When all six cameras align in the same direction, high-probability setups emerge:

| Camera Feed | Full Bullish Alignment | Full Bearish Alignment | Mean-Reversion / Rotation |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Camera 6 (Weather)** | M2 expanding, TGA draining, DXY dropping | M2 shrinking, rates rising, DXY pumping | Macro neutral, rangebound liquidity |
| **Camera 1 (Supply)** | Exchange reserves dropping, LTH holding | Exchange inflows surging, CDD spiking | Supply flat, neutral exchange flows |
| **Camera 3 (Profile)** | Price above VAH, migrating to new HVN | Price below VAL, migrating down HVN | Rotating inside Value Area (VAH to VAL) |
| **Camera 5 (Gamma)** | -Gamma short squeeze potential above key pin | -Gamma long liquidation cascade below pin | +Gamma pinning price near VPOC |
| **Camera 4 (DOM)** | Thin ask depth above, heavy bid walls below | Heavy ask walls above, thin bid depth below | Heavy bid AND ask walls clamping price |
| **Camera 2 (Aggression)**| Spot CVD surging, OI expanding organically | Aggressive market selling, CVD breakdown | CVD divergence / absorption at bounds |

## 3. Worked Scenarios

### Scenario A: The Highway Breakout
- **Weather**: M2 expanding, yields falling.
- **Supply**: Exchange reserves at multi-year lows.
- **Profile**: Price pushes out of established HVN district into an empty LVN highway.
- **Gamma**: Price passes the Zero-Gamma Flip level into -Gamma territory.
- **DOM**: Ask depth above is paper-thin.
- **Aggression**: Spot CVD explodes upward with expanding OI.
- **Outcome**: Initiative buying carries price rapidly across the highway to the next district.

### Scenario B: The Failed Migration Snapback
- **Weather**: Neutral to tight liquidity.
- **Supply**: Moderate exchange inflows.
- **Profile**: Price pokes above VAH into an LVN highway but fails to build a new HVN.
- **Gamma**: Heavy +Gamma pin sits just below VAH.
- **DOM**: Massive passive ask wall absorbs buyers at the breakout floor.
- **Aggression**: Speculative CVD (David) surges, but spot CVD lags, followed by aggressive absorption.
- **Outcome**: The migration fails; price snaps back aggressively into the middle of the old value area.
